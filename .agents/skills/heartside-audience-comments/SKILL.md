name: heartside-audience-comments
description: Pick high-reach third-party dog posts for Heartside to join and draft a reply Krish would actually leave. Use for new-audience discovery, not owned-post scheduling or existing-follower care.
---

Read [the working taste record](references/taste.md) before each pick. Update it in the same turn whenever Krish grades, rewrites, skips, approves, or posts himself.

Choose a specific post with a plausible US dog-owner audience. Check its age and actual activity. Prefer an active conversation to a bare follower count. Inspect the scene or use explicitly attributed caption details. Read liked comments and replies; an owner or foster already in the thread can make a reply more natural. Never imply you saw something the caption alone established.

Find the one detail that makes this particular dog funny. Give it a familiar human predicament. Use dry understatement, sarcasm, a dad joke or a pun if it follows naturally. Try the line aloud. If it could go under ten other posts, if the pun needs explaining, or if the joke depends on misreading what the dog is doing, discard it. Leave sincere or sensitive threads alone when there is no natural opening. Keep brand copy out of the joke.

Bring one pick with a link, exact text, and whether it is a top-level comment or a reply to someone. Draft independently. Publish only within the actual approval for that text, target and account. Verify the author and comment permalink after sending. A skip or manual post closes that candidate.

Each turn report a subjective confidence score for predicting unchanged approval, its change from the last reported score, and the specific evidence. Establish a baseline on the first report. Independent approvals count; examples supplied by Krish teach taste but do not count as independent wins. Hold on ungraded drafts and operational turns. Rejections or substantive rewrites lower confidence. Record verdicts and track future unchanged approvals out of all independently graded first picks. A score never grants publishing authority.

Keep audience outcomes separate from taste. Comment likes and replies measure reception. Account-level follows and profile visits over the same window are directional when other activity overlaps. Claim attributed followers, US geography, or sales only with evidence for that claim.
