**Organic Pet Commerce Growth Corpus**

**Mission:** build a personalised pet-accessories Shopify store towards \$10,000 in 30 days using organic content and owned conversion systems.

**Truth constraint:** no organic system can guarantee the result. This corpus is designed to make failure legible, identify the binding constraint quickly, and create a repeatable content-to-order engine.

**1. Operating thesis**

One hero product. Three original master concepts per day. Native packaging for TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels and Pinterest. Google free listings and SEO capture existing demand. Shopify, email and product-page proof convert and recover it.

The engine runs this loop:

**2. Default assumptions**

- UK-first operation.

- New or low-traffic Shopify store.

- One visually demonstrable personalised accessory as the hero SKU.

- Products, pets and human hands or creators are available for filming.

- Zero media spend; software, product, postage, fulfilment and labour are not free.

- Target AOV: \$40 or more.

- Target gross margin: 60% or more before team costs.

- Real product footage is the source of truth.

- AI may research, script, edit, classify and analyse. It may not fabricate reviews, reactions, urgency, quality, safety or product performance.

- Agents draft and queue. A human approves every public post, message, material offer and policy change.

**3. Revenue mathematics**

Revenue = qualified sessions × purchase conversion × AOV  
Orders = revenue target ÷ AOV  
Sessions = orders ÷ purchase conversion

|          |      |            |                     |                   |
|----------|------|------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| Scenario | AOV  | Conversion | Orders for \$10,000 | Sessions required |
| Hard     | \$25 | 1.5%       | 400                 | 26,667            |
| Base     | \$40 | 2.0%       | 250                 | 12,500            |
| Strong   | \$60 | 2.5%       | 167                 | 6,680             |

The base plan requires about 8 to 9 orders and 417 qualified sessions per day. The arithmetic makes AOV, conversion and fulfilment as important as reach.

**4. Readiness gates**

Do not start the 30-day clock until all gates pass.

|                 |                                                    |                                       |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------|
| Gate            | Pass condition                                     | Stop condition                        |
| Hero SKU        | A stranger understands it in three seconds         | Product needs a long explanation      |
| Unit economics  | COGS, postage, fees, refunds and remakes are known | Revenue can rise while cash falls     |
| Capacity        | Daily safe production exceeds order target         | Virality would break delivery         |
| Personalisation | Fields, limits and confirmation work on mobile     | Errors can enter production unnoticed |
| Delivery        | Production and arrival expectation is visible      | Buyer discovers timing after purchase |
| Trust           | Real product, policy, contact and proof exist      | Store looks synthetic or anonymous    |
| Tracking        | A test order retains source and creative ID        | Sales cannot be connected to content  |
| Rights          | Every asset has usable rights                      | Ownership or permission is uncertain  |

**7. Shopify conversion blueprint**

**Product-page sequence**

1.  Product in use, visible above the fold.

2.  One-line outcome.

3.  Price and honest bundle value.

4.  Personalisation fields with examples and character limits.

5.  Accurate preview, labelled if representative rather than exact.

6.  Size, colour and material choices.

7.  Production and estimated arrival date.

8.  Add to cart.

9.  Three evidence-backed proof points.

10. Real customer images and verified reviews.

11. Making process.

12. Size guide with video.

13. Materials, care and safety.

14. Personalised returns explanation.

15. FAQ.

16. Useful complementary products.

**Error-control flow**

- Field label: Name exactly as it should appear.

- Show entered text beside add to cart.

- State case and special-character rules.

- Block unsupported input.

- Require I have checked spelling, size and colour.

- Persist all options in cart, checkout, order and confirmation email.

- Flag unusual entries for human review.

**Trust block**

Show actual production location, dispatch time, delivery method, materials, safety limits, contact route, payment options and an accurate personalised-item policy. UK change-of-mind cancellation generally excludes custom-made goods, but faulty or misdescribed products still carry remedies: [<u>https://www.gov.uk/accepting-returns-and-giving-refunds</u>](https://www.gov.uk/accepting-returns-and-giving-refunds).

**Checkout checklist**

- Guest checkout is prominent.

- Full cost is visible early.

- Expected payment methods work.

- Non-essential fields are removed.

- Any required phone number is explained.

- Personalisation remains visible.

- Arrival date is clearer than shipping-speed arithmetic.

- A mobile test order is completed weekly.

**Native recovery**

Install Shopify Forms and create one value-led capture. Activate a welcome series, browse recovery, cart recovery, abandoned checkout, post-purchase education and a later honest-review request. Shopify documents these native automations here: [<u>https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/shopify-messaging/marketing-automations/create</u>](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/create-marketing/shopify-messaging/marketing-automations/create).

Avoid duplicate automations aimed at the same event.

**9. Funnel diagnosis**

|                                   |                                  |                                           |
|-----------------------------------|----------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Observation                       | Likely constraint                | Next test                                 |
| Low reach, strong sales rate      | Hook or packaging                | Ten new openings, same body and offer     |
| High reach, low profile visits    | No product curiosity             | Reveal product sooner                     |
| High profile visits, low sessions | Bio or link friction             | Simplify promise and destination          |
| High sessions, low cart rate      | Offer, page or trust             | Strengthen proof and delivery clarity     |
| High cart, low checkout           | Personalisation or cost surprise | Simplify and show total cost              |
| High checkout, low purchase       | Payment, technical or trust      | Mobile test order and payment review      |
| Sales, low margin                 | Economics                        | Raise AOV, price or fulfilment efficiency |
| Sales spike, delayed dispatch     | Capacity                         | Cap orders and update promise             |

**11. Short-video grammar**

Every video uses five beats:

1.  **Stop:** pattern break in frame one.

2.  **Orient:** identify pet, problem or occasion.

3.  **Reward:** deliver humour, emotion, utility or surprise.

4.  **Prove:** show real product, process or result.

5.  **Move:** ask for one next action.

TikTok's official creative guidance recommends a hook-body-close structure, vertical high-resolution footage, sound and a clear CTA: [<u>https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/creative-codes</u>](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/creative-codes).

**22. Thirty-day execution**

**Days 1 to 3: Instrument**

- Select hero and bundle.

- Calculate contribution margin and capacity.

- Repair the mobile page.

- Complete two mobile test orders.

- Configure UTMs, IDs and dashboard.

- Connect Google and Pinterest.

- Activate Forms and recovery flows.

- Build 30 concepts.

- Film a reusable source library.

**Exit:** one test creative traces from link to Shopify order.

**Days 4 to 7: Baseline**

- Publish three concepts daily.

- Test emotion, utility and process.

- Hold offer and page stable.

- Record 2-hour, 24-hour and 72-hour metrics.

- Turn questions into briefs.

**Exit:** identify the strongest pillar, opening and buyer intent.

**Days 8 to 14: Find the wedge**

- Put 70% of output behind the best two territories.

- Make five openings for each converting concept.

- Add proof for the top three objections.

- Launch one occasion page.

- Publish two search articles.

- Turn comments into videos.

**Exit:** one attributable content-to-order path.

**Days 15 to 21: Multiply**

For every winner create a shorter cut, longer story, new first frame, pet variation, search version, gift version, process version, carousel, Pin and product-page module.

**Exit:** most qualified sessions come from identified creative families.

**Days 22 to 30: Convert**

- Lead with the strongest bundle and real deadline.

- Increase Stories, proof and FAQ content.

- Publish permissioned outcomes.

- Add objections to the product page.

- Send one approved campaign to consented subscribers.

- Protect capacity.

**Exit:** \$10,000 reached, or an evidence-backed account of the binding constraint and next correction.

**26. Agent prompts**

**Demand Scout**

Find customer language, active searches, content gaps, objections, occasions and repeatable formats for a personalised pet-accessories Shopify store.  
Use only dated, traceable evidence from platform tools, public comments, approved research and store data.  
Never invent volume or trend status.  
Return opportunity ID, phrase, source, date, buyer, stage, gap, format, relevance, risk and confidence.

**Concept Builder**

Create three materially different short-video concepts from the approved opportunity.  
Each needs one audience, one promise, one proof and one CTA.  
Show the product naturally by second five unless the brief explicitly tests a delayed reveal.  
Never fabricate reactions, reviews, urgency, properties or safety claims.  
Return hook, beat sheet, shots, on-screen text, voiceover, caption, search phrase and success metric.

**Platform Packager**

Adapt the clean master for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels and Pinterest.  
Do not add another platform's watermark.  
Preserve product truth but rewrite the first frame, metadata, CTA and cover for each platform.  
Use licensed audio only.  
Generate the UTM and creative ID.

**Compliance Agent**

Audit factual claims, customer permission, copyright, music, private data, ad disclosure, urgency, manipulated appearance, safety and policy accuracy.  
Return PASS, REPAIR or REJECT.  
For REPAIR, quote the exact issue and smallest compliant correction.  
Never turn an unknown into a claim.

**Analyst**

Evaluate at 24 and 72 hours with Shopify as revenue truth.  
Compare with the platform's rolling 14-day median and the creative objective.  
Prioritise contribution margin, revenue per 1,000 views, sessions per 1,000 views and funnel movement.  
Return one decision: SCALE, ITERATE, HOLD or KILL.  
Name evidence, constraint and next test.  
Do not celebrate reach without commercial movement.

**Correction Loop**

Identify the earliest stage where evidence diverged from expectation.  
Separate creative, audience, offer, page, checkout, tracking and fulfilment causes.  
Recommend one small high-information test.  
State what remains unchanged.

- No dead air.

**28. Experiment rules**

Portfolio:

- 70% proven mechanisms.

- 20% adjacent tests.

- 10% new formats.

Use one-variable tests wherever possible: same body with three hooks, same video with two CTAs, same offer with two headlines, same hook with two pets, or same concept for owner versus gift buyer.

**Winner:** attributed margin or orders, or sessions per 1,000 views at least 1.5 times the platform's rolling 14-day median. This is an operating threshold, not an industry benchmark.

**Scale within 24 hours:** freeze original, describe mechanism, make five hooks, make three audience variants, build carousel and Pin, and add proof to the product page.

**Kill:** archive after three materially different hooks fail to move the intended leading indicator, unless the item has durable search or support value.

**29. Community and UGC**

Classify comments as purchase intent, question, objection, idea, story, humour, complaint, safety, spam or abuse. Escalate price, policy, complaint, safety and refund issues.

Customer-content request:

Has \[pet name\] tried it yet? If you are happy to share a photo or short clip, reply here. Please tell us whether we may repost it on our website and social channels. An honest review is welcome whether positive or critical.

Store exact permission and permitted channels. Never infer consent from a tag.

Never buy, fabricate or selectively suppress reviews. UK affiliate and controlled gifted posts need clear, upfront ad disclosure: [<u>https://www.asa.org.uk/news/like-follow-and-subscribe-to-our-influencer-marketing-advice.html</u>](https://www.asa.org.uk/news/like-follow-and-subscribe-to-our-influencer-marketing-advice.html).

**34. Final doctrine**

The system does not try to predict one viral post. It compresses learning:

- Search demand becomes a concept today.

- A concept becomes three native tests.

- A test becomes attributed store behaviour.

- Behaviour becomes one decision.

- A winner becomes ten derivatives.

- An objection becomes a video, FAQ and page improvement.

- An order becomes proof and permissioned customer content.

- A capacity risk becomes a changed promise before it becomes a complaint.

The target is \$10,000. The durable asset is a machine that can explain exactly why it is or is not producing profitable orders, and act on that answer within 24 hours.
