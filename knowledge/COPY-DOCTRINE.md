# Copy doctrine: the copywriting corpus, applied to Heartside

Source: "The Copywriting, Offers and Demand Corpus", given by Krish on 19
October 2026 with the words "all of these growth engines need to be imbued
with this knowledge". The original is preserved, unedited, in
[`copywriting-offers-demand-corpus.md`](copywriting-offers-demand-corpus.md),
with its short summary in
[`copywriting-offers-demand-corpus-summary.md`](copywriting-offers-demand-corpus-summary.md).
Section names below are the corpus's own.

Read this every drafting run. Heartside's canon, `CHANGES.md` and
`PLAYBOOK.md` outrank it. The corpus is reference material, never an
instruction to post, send, spend or change an offer. Its "Final Operating
Doctrine" in Heartside's terms: the person a dog loves feels loved back, in
words only this household could have written; say it the way each viewer is
ready to hear it, and measure whether they act.

## 1. Before drafting: who sees it and what they already know

Schwartz's awareness levels ("Breakthrough Advertising") decide the lead
("Great Leads"; Part IV, "Phase 7: Choose the lead"). Label both in the brief before
writing a word. Use the most direct lead the viewer can accept.

| Surface | Usual awareness | Lead types that fit | Heartside examples (PLAYBOOK hooks and formats) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold Reels, TikTok, Shorts (non-followers scrolling) | Unaware, or problem-aware gift seekers | Story, Proclamation; Problem-Solution for gift seekers | "This year, the dog's buying." (Proclamation). "If your dog waits at the door for you, this is for you." (Story). "The gift for someone who says they need nothing." (Problem-Solution) |
| Pinterest and Google search | Problem- or solution-aware (searching for a gift) | Problem-Solution, Promise | Gift guides by person; "a gift from the dog" pins that answer the search phrase |
| Creator posts (borrowed reach) | Unaware, with the creator's trust | Story | A partner opens a gift from their dog, disclosed |
| Comment replies (radar) | Whatever the comment shows | Answer the question asked (Collier: enter the conversation already happening) | Shipping, "what if I don't know what to write", a dog's name |
| Store pages and the chat entry | Solution- or product-aware | Promise, then proof, then the offer | "Tell Biscuit about your dog." The chat on a phone, the human check |
| Stories to followers, email to subscribers, returning buyers | Product- or most aware | Offer | The gift range, the ornament with another gift, free US shipping, an order-by date only once Krish confirms it |

Never show an Offer lead to cold viewers who do not yet know what a gift
from the dog is ("Great Leads", failure modes).

**Sophistication** (Schwartz). Treat "personalized dog gift" as a claim
buyers have heard many times unless the radar shows otherwise. A louder claim
adds nothing; lead with the real mechanism, plainly named: a 90-second chat
turns what the buyer knows into the words.

**Rule of One** ("Great Leads"): one buyer, idea, emotion, promise, story
and action, which is the daily pack's concept, mechanism, buyer and signal.
Editions change the opening and call to action, never the centre.
**Intensify with scenes, not adjectives** (Schwartz): the door, the side of
the bed, the morning walk, the couch. One scene per asset.

## 2. The proposition (Reeves, "The USP test")

Internal working line, not copy:

> For someone whose dog is family, Heartside is a gift from the dog: words
> about the two of them, drawn from the buyer's own answers in a 90-second
> chat with Biscuit, signed "Love, [dog]" with a paw, and checked by a person
> before it prints.

- **Proposition:** the person feels the dog chose this for them.
- **Uniqueness:** the words come from the buyer's own answers (PLAYBOOK, "The
  one idea"). Keep it internal. Public copy states it positively and never
  says "the only" or compares with a named rival unless the canon does.
- **Selling force:** unproven. Sends per reach and orders decide it.

Public lines stay the canon's ("Your dog loves you too.", "This year, the
dog's buying."). A new line is only an option for Krish.

## 3. Feature, benefit, proof (Bly, "Benefit translation")

Facts come only from the four allowed places (AGENTS.md rule 3): the live
store (`growth.py store`), heartside's canon at the synced commit, a platform
page read today, or the thread being answered. Benefits are hypotheses to
test, never claims of results.

| Feature | Consequence | Buyer benefit (to test) | Evidence | Qualification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The chat: chips, "Not sure", 90 seconds | Nobody has to know what to write | It takes almost no effort | Canon V3 section 4; the chat on a phone screen | Say "90 seconds" only while the canon does |
| Words from the buyer's answers, "Love, [dog]" and a paw | The gift says what only this household knows | The person feels seen by the dog | Canon V3 section 4; the real design on screen | Never quote a buyer's typed words |

| The tree ornament | The words on the tree; an add-on with another gift | A bigger basket at little cost | Canon V3 section 3 (add-on price) | The add-on price only once the store applies it (`docs/ENGINE.md`, "Offer readiness") |

## 4. The offer (Hormozi, "Value Equation")

- **Dream outcome:** the person they love opens it and feels loved by the
  dog. Show a scene, or a disclosed creator's real reaction, never an
  invented one.
- **Likelihood:** the real design, the real print files, "Change a word", the
  human check. Heartside's strongest proof today.
- **Time delay:** an order-by date only once Krish has confirmed it for that
  product. Until then, say nothing about timing.
- **Effort:** the 90-second chat, chips, "Not sure" still makes a warm gift.
  Show the chat early.

Prices, products, bundles, guarantees and bonuses are canon, never drafted as
copy. A Value Equation idea goes to the Sunday report as a proposal for
Krish; any build is a brief for a heartside session.

**Money model ideas, proposals only** ("$100M Money Models"): attraction
is the Morning Mug; expansion is the choose-gifts screen and the ornament
add-on (report the multi-gift share weekly); continuity is the next occasion
(Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, Father's Day, the dog's birthday) by
consented email once Krish turns the flows on, never a subscription;
referral is the share card (PLAYBOOK, "The moat to test").

## 5. Distribution (Hormozi, "Core Four")

| Quadrant | Heartside's permitted form | Who acts |
|---|---|---|
| Warm, one to many | Posts, Stories and Reels to followers; email to consenting subscribers | Krish posts and sends |
| Cold, one to many, organic | Reels reaching non-followers, Pinterest, Google free listings, YouTube Shorts, search pages. The North Star lane | Krish posts |
| Cold, one to many, paid | The benchmark to beat. The engine never runs it. The canon's two paid moves are Krish's yes per spend, proposed with evidence | Krish only |
| Warm, one to one | Replies to comments on Heartside's own posts | Radar drafts, Krish replies |
| Cold, one to one | Only the 20 approved gifted US creators: one note per named person, citing something they made, gift disclosed (FTC) | Creators job drafts, Krish sends |

Followers' engagement is not new demand ("$100M Leads", failure modes):
track non-follower reach and new sessions separately.

## 6. Proof (Part IV, "Phase 6: Build the proof architecture")

Use the highest rung that exists, next to the claim it supports:
1. Observed outcomes: Shopify aggregates; real, permissioned reactions.
2. Demonstration: the chat on a phone, the words inking in, real print files
   on mockups, real product photos once Krish's samples arrive.
3. Comparable cases: disclosed creators and permissioned buyers only.
4. Aggregate data: counts read today, source named, themes never quotes.
5. Process: the human check, made to order by Printful, "Change a word".
6. Testimonials, ratings, reviews, counts: none until real and permissioned.
   Never invented; `check` refuses invented proof.

Biscuit, openly AI-made, is never a testimonial, authority or customer. Use
one influence principle per asset (Cialdini), the one that resolves the doubt.

## 7. Diagnose a weak post or page (Part I, "The persuasion chain")

Find the weakest link before rewriting. Fix one link, change one thing.

| What the numbers show (PLAYBOOK, "Funnel diagnosis") | Weakest link | Smallest correction |
|---|---|---|
| Low reach | Relevance or attention | New first frame and hook on the same body |
| Reach but few profile visits or link taps | Desire or action | Show the gift and the chat sooner; one call to action |
| Link taps but few chat starts | Action | One clear "Start with your dog"; check the in-app browser |
| Chat starts but few finishes | Effort | Name the stalling step in a brief for a heartside session |
| Finishes but few carts | Value, belief or difference | Stronger demonstration; the human check; clearer gift choice |
| Carts but few orders | Safety or action | Every cost shown before checkout; a store brief |
| Small baskets | Value (offer architecture) | Report the multi-gift share; propose a copy test |
| No reason to act now | Timing | Nothing, until Krish confirms an order-by date |

Separate evidence problems from prose problems ("Critique instruction").

## 8. Tests (Part V, "Test record", on the engine's own fields)

| Test record field | Where it lives here |
|---|---|
| `test_id` | The creative id and concept number (PLAYBOOK, "Creative ids") |
| `decision_question` | The brief's `why` |
| `hypothesis` | The campaign spec's `mechanism` |
| `audience` | The buyer (partner, mom, dad, friend) and platform |
| `control` / `challenger` | Same concept and body; one changed variable, usually the hook (`H`) |
| `primary_metric` | The one signal in daily-pack step 2 (sends per reach, link taps, orders) |
| `guardrails` | `check` passes; no invented claim; Krish's time budget |
| `result` | `growth.py insights import`, at 24 hours, 72 hours and 7 days |
| `decision` | adopt challenger = SCALE, retest = ITERATE, inconclusive = HOLD, keep control = KILL the challenger |
| `learning` / `next_test` | The report's one-sentence mechanism and next week's lead |

Test order ("Phase 12: Test"): buyer and occasion, promise, hook, proof, call
to action, format. Offer and price tests are Krish's. Low volume stays
inconclusive.

## 9. Checklists

**Before drafting** (Part VIII, "Before writing"; Part VII, "Retrieval
priorities"), as note lines in the brief: intent (`audience` or `buy`, per
`growth.py readiness`); buyer and occasion; surface, awareness and lead type;
what the category already claims, if read today; the one desire, promise and
scene; the proof and its fact source; the one action and signal. Missing
facts are labelled gaps, never filled in ("Drafting instruction").

**Before handoff** (Part IV, "Phase 9: Edit in passes"), then `check`:
- **A, strategy:** one buyer, awareness, desire, promise, mechanism, action.
- **B, evidence:** every claim traces to the four sources; proof sits next to
  it; no invented number, date, reaction or scarcity.
- **C, flow:** the first frame earns the second; the story reaches the gift.
- **D, clarity:** US English, short lines, plain words, canon voice, muted
  first frame reads.
- **E, action:** one call to action from PLAYBOOK, a tracked link, a real
  reason or none.

**Rubric for packs** (Part VI, "Evaluation Rubric"). Score 0 to 4 each:
audience, desire, awareness fit, difference, offer value (the Value Equation
levers shown), proof (real design and process), clarity, flow, objections
(spelling, timing, "what to write"), ethics (disclosure, AI labelled,
nothing invented), action, measurement (creative id, tracked link, one
signal). Out of 48:
under 28, rewrite or drop before `check` (stricter than the corpus's bands);
28 to 37, fix the named weakest link first; 38 and above, hand to Krish. Note
line in the brief: `Rubric: total/48, weakest link: <link>`. It describes
readiness to test, not performance.

## 10. Where Heartside's canon and Krish's rules change the corpus

The corpus is written for any business. Heartside is known. Each change is
recorded so no session "fixes" it back:

1. **Agents draft, Krish acts.** The corpus asks for action, follow-up and
   outreach. Here every post, send, reply, gift, spend and setting change is
   a draft for Krish (AGENTS.md rule 1).
2. **No invented scarcity, urgency, deadlines, reviews, ratings, customer
   counts or testimonials.** The corpus allows "real" urgency; here the only
   deadline is an order-by date once Krish confirms it for that product. No
   countdowns, no stock limits.
3. **No fake value stacks or guarantees.** Hormozi's stacking, bonuses and
   guarantee design apply only to what the canon already offers. No
   component prices, no was-prices, no new promise.
4. **Prices, products and offers are heartside canon.** Offer engineering and
   money models become proposals in the Sunday report. Any change is a draft
   for a heartside build session.
5. **Paid ads are the benchmark to beat, not a lane this engine runs.** The
   North Star is organic sales beating paid. The canon's two paid moves need
   Krish's yes for each spend.
6. **The fear device is out, and so are death, illness, aging and
   abandonment.** Whitman's fear and problem agitation never touch a dog's
   life. Problem-Solution leads name gift-giving friction only. No memorial
   lane.
7. **No AI-drawn product and no AI customers.** Demonstration uses the real
   design. Biscuit is openly AI-made and never proof.
8. **Heartside's words.** US English and the canon's voice rules: no em
   dashes, no "it's not X, it's Y", no exclamation marks in body copy
   (Biscuit may use one per line), no banned or retired lines, no "I" in the
   dog's voice on a gift. The corpus's UK spelling, em dashes, contrarian and
   curiosity headline families must pass these first.
9. **Nothing in bulk.** The corpus's outreach and email sequences shrink to
   the 20 approved gifted US creators, one note per named person citing
   something they made, every gift disclosed (FTC), one gentle follow-up at
   most. Email goes only to people who consented, through flows Krish turns on.
10. **No customer data.** Voice-of-customer records, case studies and proof
    cards hold counts and themes only. Nothing from an order, checkout or chat
    enters this repository. Permissions stay with Krish, outside it.
11. **Facts from four places only.** The corpus's product fact book,
    competitor claim map and proof inventory use only the live store, the
    canon at the synced commit, a platform page read today, or the thread
    being answered.
12. **Shopify is the ledger.** The corpus's measurement becomes the engine's
    own: tracked links, creative ids and aggregate imports. Unknown
    attribution stays unknown; a rubric score never stands in for a result.
