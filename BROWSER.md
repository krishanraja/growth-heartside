# Browser access: read with Krish's logins, never act

Krish may run this engine with a browser signed in to Heartside's accounts.
That lets a job read what only he can see: Shopify's reports, Meta's
insights, TikTok Studio, Pinterest and Google Merchant Center. The browser is
for **reading only**.

## The line you never cross

In a signed-in browser you may open pages, scroll, search, sort and filter,
open analytics and reports, and copy numbers out. You may **never**:

- type into a post, caption, comment, reply, message, review or chat box,
  not even to "prepare" a draft there (drafts live in the pack);
- press Post, Publish, Share, Send, Reply, Like, Follow, Boost, Promote,
  Schedule, Save, Install, Add app, Confirm, Approve, Fulfill, Refund,
  Archive, Delete or Pay;
- **confirm, edit or cancel a Printful order, or place a Shopify order.**
  Printful is set to manual confirm so that a person checks every order
  before it prints. That person is Krish, never you;
- change a theme, a product, a price, a discount, a shipping or market
  setting, a profile, a bio, a link, a catalog, a feed or an automation;
- accept or decline anything (invitations, terms, cookies beyond what is
  needed to read), or sign in to an account Krish is not already signed in to.

If a page shows a pop-up that needs one of these, close it or leave the page.
If a task seems to need one, write it as a draft in the pack, with exactly
where to click, for Krish to do.

If an account shows you signed out, don't sign in. Say "signed out of
<place>" in the pack's headline and carry on with what is public.

## Customer details stay where they are

Shopify's orders, customers and abandoned checkouts show people's names,
emails, addresses, their dogs' names and photos, and the words they wrote.
Read **reports**, not records. If you open an order to check a number, copy
nothing from it except counts. Nothing that identifies a buyer, a recipient
or their dog goes into a pack or a file (AGENTS.md, rule 6).

## Where to read, place by place

| Place | Signed-in pages to read | What to take |
|---|---|---|
| Shopify | Analytics; Reports: sessions over time, sessions by referrer, sales over time, sales by traffic referrer, marketing (by utm campaign and content), conversion over time | Orders, gross and net sales, average order value, sessions, sessions by source, add-to-cart and checkout rates, sales by utm_content (the creative id) |
| Shopify, orders list (counts only) | Orders filtered by day | How many orders, how many with two or more gifts, how many waiting to be checked |
| Meta Business Suite | Insights for Instagram @yourdoghasnotes and the Facebook Page | Followers; reach per Reel; sends (shares), saves and comments per Reel; profile visits; link taps; audience by country |
| TikTok Studio | Analytics for @yourdoghasnotes | Followers; views per post; average watch time; audience by country; Creator Search Insights topics |
| Pinterest | Analytics, once the business account exists | Impressions, saves and outbound clicks per Pin |
| Google Merchant Center | Products > Diagnostics; Performance | Disapproved products and why; clicks from free listings |
| n8n | The data table "Heartside AI calls" | The share of chat calls that fell back (`source = fallback` or `slow = true`), as a count over all rows |
| Printful | The dashboard's production and shipping times (read only) | Current production time per product, for the order-by date Krish confirms |

For the radar, also read in public: TikTok search, Pinterest search,
Instagram hashtag and search pages, Google's suggestions for gift phrases,
Reddit threads where dog owners talk about gifts, and the comments on
Heartside's own posts. The same rules apply: read, never act.

## If no signed-in browser is available

Use public pages (`jobs/numbers.md` lists them) and say in the pack's
headline that the dashboards were not available. Never fill a gap with an
estimate.
