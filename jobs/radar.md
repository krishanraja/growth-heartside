# Job: radar (every day)

Kind `radar`. Part 1 in `PLAYBOOK.md`: demand and the buyer's words. Time for
Krish: about 5 minutes.

1. `python3 growth.py sync`. Use the `heartside_sha` it prints.
2. **Comments on Heartside's own posts** (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, in the
   signed-in browser or in public; read only). Sort each useful comment into:
   - purchase intent;
   - a question;
   - an objection;
   - a dog's name (the comment loop);
   - a story;
   - a complaint;
   - spam.

   Draft a reply for each one worth answering, up to 10, best first:
   - `platform`: where the comment is;
   - `target_url`: the post;
   - `target_name`: the commenter's public handle;
   - `text`: the reply, in Heartside's voice. Answer the question the
     commenter asked, in their terms (`knowledge/COPY-DOCTRINE.md`, section
     1, comment replies).

   A "Comment your dog's name" reply is in Heartside's plain, warm voice, uses
   the commenter's dog's name only as they wrote it, and points to the bio
   link. No exclamation marks or emoji. Escalate price, policy, complaint
   and anything sad to Krish as an item with `platform: other`, `why`
   "escalate", and no drafted reply. The two first-follower comments Krish
   approved on 7 Nov carry an emoji: that approval covers those two exact
   comments only, never this rule.

   **Engagement tests and first followers.** Run `python3 growth.py social
   status`. For every open test, do the next action it names, and only if you
   own it (`engagement` is this job; `publishing` is the publishing desk). When
   a test's 48-hour window has closed, count the unique substantive replies
   (brand activity excluded), keep verified-follower replies apart from
   aggregate ones, and record the outcome as an `evaluated` social event with
   its decision (keep, iterate, hold or retire) through `growth.py events
   import` (`docs/ENGINE.md`, "Social history"). A replied-to test feeds the
   next concept: what the replies said goes into today's demand items. For a
   channel the first-follower monitor still watches, read its follower count
   and record it as a `follower_check`; a page that will not load is
   `unavailable`, never 0. At the first verified count of 1 or more, draft one
   engagement test for that channel: the live post, the exact comment, the
   interaction sought, the viewer's route to heartside.io, the 48-hour window
   and the decision rule, as an item for Krish.
3. **Demand.** In public, look for what people search and say about gifts
   from dogs, dog gifts for a partner, mom or dad, and the season's gifts:
   - TikTok search and Creator Search Insights (when signed in);
   - Pinterest search suggestions;
   - Google's suggestions;
   - Instagram search;
   - Reddit threads where dog owners talk about gifts (read only, never post).

   For up to 5 opportunities, write an item with `platform: other`:
   - `text`: the exact phrase or question, where you saw it (url), the date,
     the buyer (partner, mom, dad, friend, self), the awareness level and
     lead type it suggests (`knowledge/COPY-DOCTRINE.md`, section 1), any
     competing promise seen on that page, the content gap, a suggested
     format from the PLAYBOOK bank, and your confidence (high, medium or
     low);
   - `why`: one line.

   Never claim a search volume or a trend you did not read.
   **Weekly, on Mondays: trend-scan and idea-bank** (docs/CHANNEL-TASKS.md).
   For Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, add one `platform: other` item each:
   - trend-scan: 10 opportunities (trends, audience interests, new platform
     features, formats that are working), each with its source url and the
     date read, ranked by fit, originality, demand and ease, and how to adapt
     it to Heartside without copying the creator;
   - idea-bank: 30 ideas from the buyers' problems, questions, mistakes and
     misconceptions seen above, each with an angle, an opening hook, a format
     from the PLAYBOOK bank, the core value, why someone would save or send
     it, and the signal it serves (sends, link taps or orders).
   A trend or claim with no source or date is dropped.
4. **The buyer's own words.** If the store numbers show orders, note in one
   item which chat answers are most common (chip counts only, never a
   buyer's typed words). These feed the daily pack's hooks.
5. Headline: for example "Radar: 6 replies (2 asking about shipping), 3
   openings: 'gift from the dog to mom'". Check, then post.
