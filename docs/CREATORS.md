# Creator acquisition operating brief

The allowance is 20 gifted US creators. It is a cap, not 20 gifts already
ordered, 20 named creators already approved or 20 guaranteed posts. No paid
placement, purchase, message or product order is automated.

Keep real handles, public post URLs, source observations and individual drafts
under ignored `local/lists/`, outside engine checkpoints and GitHub artifacts.
Public comments do not establish permission to reuse a person's image or words.

For each candidate, verify a current public profile and one relevant post, US
location or explicit US delivery eligibility, fit with partner/family dog gifting,
and recent audience behavior. Do not infer US location from English, a breed or
a profile picture. Record which facts are verified and which are pending. Never
copy a creator's contact details from a scraped list. Use their public business
contact surface by hand.

Draft a short individual note citing the exact observed content. Offer a gift
only once the public product and checkout are verified. No promise of a free
order, guaranteed post, invented reaction or paid usage rights. A reply is not
an order confirmation.

When a creator accepts, Krish records permission and shipping privately outside
this repository. Record only the anonymous creator ID, stage, creative ID,
tracked link, gifted cost and public post URL in aggregate reporting. Krish
places the gift order and confirms printing. Request permission separately for
organic reuse and paid usage. Disclose gifted product clearly in the post; do
not rely on a buried tag. Recheck the current FTC guidance before execution.

A creator brief should ask for a specific ordinary ritual between the dog and
the recipient, the real approved gift, and the creator's own honest response.
Never script a fabricated testimonial. Suggested structure: 2-second ritual,
real product reveal, readable detail, their own words, one tracked next action.
The creator remains free to decline or give an honest review.

Stop expanding if the gift range is not purchasable, margin is unknown, the
20-creator allowance is exhausted or the first cohort shows no qualified US
visits. Do not use reach or a gifted placement as proof of contribution.
