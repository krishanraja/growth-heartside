## What the numbers say

**1. Nothing has yet produced buying interest at scale.** Two orders in 60 days (one $24 gross, $0 net, week of
12 Oct; one $45, week of 2 Nov). Across all 104 posts the high-intent actions (shares, saves, follows, clicks and
comments) total 12: Instagram 4, TikTok 6, Facebook 2.

**2. Instagram is the only social source with buying intent.** Store sessions by referrer, 45 days: Instagram 50
sessions, 2 with a cart add, 1 completed checkout. TikTok 23 sessions, 0 cart adds. Facebook 478 sessions, 0 cart
adds. Direct 825 sessions, 62 cart adds (includes Krish's own testing; not attributable).

**3. Genuine interest: the deadpan "rule-breaking dog with an alibi" family, 12 to 14 Nov.** Reactions, comments,
shares and saves per 1,000 views, against each channel's median (Instagram 29, TikTok 40):

| Post (first line) | Channel | Views | Per 1,000 | Also |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First snow of the year. The snowman lasted twenty minutes. | TikTok | 261 | 126 | 3.6 s average watch |
| Somebody unrolled the whole roll. He has an alibi. | TikTok | 260 | 115 | |
| Effective immediately, the cat runs this department. | Instagram | 133 | 83 | 1 share |
| The gingerbread house was left unsupervised for one minute. | Instagram / TikTok | 126 / 279 | 79 / 68 | 2 shares on TikTok |
| Clean sheets day. He would like it noted that he was never on the bed. | Instagram | 125 | 72 | 1 save; best Facebook post by rate (25) |

Why, my read: each shows the dog's evidence first and then the inflated human explanation, which is exactly the
"I'd send this to ___ because ___" test in `docs/CHANNEL-TASKS.md` and the stupid-premise, cruel-payoff core in
`docs/HUMOUR.md`. The earlier gift-led films (5 to 8 Nov) reached similar numbers but earned fewer reactions.
Average watch stays at 2 to 5 seconds on every channel, so the opening shot, not the ending, is what decides.

**4. Reach without interest.** TikTok "Up at 4. Aligned with stakeholders by 5:32" (F38, 11 Nov): 2,314 views, 22
times the channel median, but 17 reactions per 1,000 (under half the median) and the lowest average watch of any
post, 1.7 s. Whether it was promoted is not in any record here; Codex reads TikTok Promote to settle it.

**5. Paid Meta traffic bought visits, not buyers.**
- The only boost in this repository's record is the F10 pilot, $25, 6 to 7 Nov (`CHANGES.md`, 13 Nov). In that
  window Shopify shows 85 Facebook-referred sessions, all landing on the mug page the pilot linked to, and 0 cart
  adds: about $0.29 a visit (inferred from Shopify; Ads Manager has the exact spend and landing page views).
- A second burst of 379 Facebook-referred sessions on 11 to 13 Nov landed on the homepage, with 0 cart adds.
  Heartside's own Facebook posts reached at most 232 people a day then (organic median reach 2), and no boost is
  recorded for those days. Krish says he has run boosts on several platforms. **The record and the traffic
  disagree**: this is a reconciliation finding, not a conclusion, until Codex reads Ads Manager.
- Facebook organic is close to dead: median reach 2 over 39 posts.

**What is worth paying to boost: nothing yet proven.** No paid visit has produced a cart add. Under the 10 Nov
ruling (at most $20 a day, one post at a time, proved by its organic reach results), the first post that earns a
boost is one that has already shown organic sends or saves, not one that has only been seen.

## Proposals for Krish (each a Yes or No; none is acted on before it)

1. **Lead next week with the alibi family.** Ten derivatives of "He has an alibi" / "He has read the rule very
   carefully", every one ending on the real product, alternating with gift-led posts as `docs/CHANNEL-TASKS.md`
   says.
2. **A boost bar.** A post may be proposed for a boost only when, at 72 hours organic, it is at least twice its
   channel's median per 1,000 and has at least one share or save; the boost links to the product page shown, and
   stops at 100 paid landing visits with no cart add. Candidates today, if the 72 h read holds: "First snow"
   (TikTok Spark) or "cat runs this department" (Instagram). No spend is proposed here.
3. **Facebook stays a paired cross-post only**, with no effort of its own, until it shows organic reach.
4. **Instagram's path to the product first.** It is the only channel that produced a cart add: check the bio
   link lands on the product the post shows, and put the next week's gift-led posts there first.
5. **Read every boost before judging any channel** (the list below), and keep paid sessions out of the organic
   number for each boost's window (`META_UNVERIFIED`, `docs/PAID-BOOSTS.md`).
