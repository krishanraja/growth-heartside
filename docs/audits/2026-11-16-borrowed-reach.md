# Borrowed reach: the fastest paid and free ways to put Heartside in front of US dog owners (16 Nov 2026)

Asked by Krish on 16 Nov 2026 ("Anything is on the table", paid included, every spend and every outreach message
with his yes first). Research only: nothing was sent, signed up for, bought or booked. Read on 16 Nov 2026 unless a
row says otherwise.

North Star part this moves: reach and visits to heartside.io from new US dog owners before the 4 Dec Christmas
date. **Any sale that paid reach brings does not count toward the North Star number** ("$0 of paid ads behind
them", `docs/NORTH_STAR.md`), so every paid placement below carries its own UTM (`utm_medium=paid_shoutout` or
`paid_social`) and is reported apart from organic.

Spend on this research: about $0.57 of Apify runs (4 runs: Instagram profiles, Instagram reels twice, TikTok
profiles; counted at the actors' listed per-result prices). Run and dataset IDs are in
`local/lists/2026-11-16-borrowed-reach-creators.md`, not here, because the datasets name creators.

**Labels used below.** *Measured*: read today from the platform through Apify (the median of each account's
latest six reels or videos). *Seller*: stated by the marketplace listing or the account itself. *Estimate*:
anything else, including my own assumptions, which say what they rest on.

Personal dog creators are coded Creator A to I (this repository is public and never names a creator; NOW.md
`never_publish`). Public meme and media pages are named. The key is in `local/lists/`.

## The short answer

1. **The cheapest reach is not where the listings say it is.** Shoutcart's own "average views" were stale or
   inflated on four of the five dog pages I checked against the platform today, by up to 48 times. Nine of the
   cheapest-looking pages have not posted in months or years. Book only from measured numbers.
2. **The best measured views per dollar are three animal pages on Shoutcart**: cuteanimalshow, cub.animals and
   petsprestige: about 1,700 to 2,100 views per dollar. But only 13 to 15% of their audience is in the US
   (Shoutcart's audience data), so that is about 260 to 320 US views per dollar.
3. **The two pages that could move the needle in one post are WeRateDogs and Barked**: median 1.38M and 0.89M plays
   per reel this month (measured). Neither publishes a price. Ask for a quote now; it will likely be above $1,000.
4. **Personal dog creators are 10 to 1,000 times worse on views per dollar** (2 to 35 views per dollar measured),
   need the physical gift before they film, and Krish ruled on 10 Nov "I'm not going to creators until you make a
   success out of this strategy". They are listed for completeness and left out of the test plans.
5. **Paid link clicks are the most predictable traffic**: Meta $0.29 to $1.45 a visit (Heartside's own pilot to
   the US November median), TikTok Spark Ads need $20 a day per ad group at minimum.
6. **Free levers that fit a new account**: Trial Reels (already approved, eligibility to confirm), TikTok search
   keywords in the spoken line, on-screen text and caption, and linked Pins in the busiest gift-search season.

## 4. Pinterest

- **All Pins can carry links.** Pinterest merged Idea Pins into standard Pins in May 2023 and opened links on every
  Pin ([TechCrunch, 11 May 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/05/11/pinterest-is-combining-pins-and-idea-pins-into-a-single-format/);
  [Pinterest](https://create.pinterest.com/blog/new-pin-format-update)). So the choice is video Pin versus image Pin,
  both linked straight to the product page with UTMs. Editing a link after publishing can reset a Pin's reactions
  ([louisem.com](https://louisem.com/395169/pinterest-idea-pins); estimate).
- **Gift intent is highest now.** Pinterest says someone searches for a gift on Pinterest every six seconds from
  October to December ([Pinterest newsroom](https://newsroom.pinterest.com/news/pinterest-holiday-edit-brings-precision-to-festive-shopping/));
  its 2025 holiday playbook says 64% of Pinners make holiday wishlists
  ([Pinterest Business](https://business.pinterest.com/pdf/holiday-playbook-2025)). Platform's own claims.
- **Heartside today:** 13 Pins, 12 of them video Pins under four days old with 0 impressions
  (`docs/audits/2026-11-16-buffer-all-posts.md`). Organic Pins take weeks to be found; 4 Dec is 18 days away.
- **Promoted Pins:** no official daily minimum found. Third-party guides say none is enforced and suggest $5 to
  $20 a day ([Tailwind](https://www.tailwindapp.com/blog/pinterest-ads-complete-beginners-guide)); minimum bids are
  $0.10 per click and $5.00 per thousand impressions ([Strike Social](https://strikesocial.com/?p=384)). Typical
  CPC $0.50 to $1.50 ([WebFX](https://www.webfx.com/social-media/how-much-does-pinterest-advertising-cost.html)).
  All estimates; Ads Manager shows the live floor.
