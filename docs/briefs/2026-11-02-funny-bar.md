# The funny bar: what Krish approved, and what it teaches

Read before drafting any funny-tone Heartside post. Source: his votes and comments on the idea board,
2 Nov 2026 (`docs/briefs/2026-11-01-ideas.json`), and his answers the same day (`CHANGES.md`).

He approved 10 of the 16 funny ideas and voted on none of the warm or pure-heart ones yet. Every comment
was about sound or comic execution: "the audio treatment and the specifics of the humor execution are
quite important to me".

## What a funny Heartside idea has

1. **A genre parody with a real sound.** Spy heist (F01, F03), wildlife documentary (F04), courtroom (F05),
   gallery critic (F07), action thriller (F12). The parody lives in the score and the voice, and the dog
   plays it completely straight.
2. **The dog's inner voice.** "What the dog's thinking in his head ... inner monologue" (F02). One voice
   per breed group, recurring, so each breed becomes a character (ruling, 2 Nov).
3. **Setup, escalation, button.** F01's setup is "a frame of the human ignoring the dog first". The
   escalation is the dog's wounded logic, and the button is the leap. The music swells into the button
   and cuts dead on it.
4. **Exaggeration you can see.** "Tiptoeing and comedy exaggeration" (F03), "obviously smug" (F07),
   "comedy caricature" (F10). A joke carried only by a caption fails.
5. **Music doing the acting.** "The music has to make this one" (F12), "comedy music" (F11).
6. **The product is the punchline.** Each one ends on the real design, labelled Example.

## Limits that stay

- The swear is bleeped, never spoken (ruling, 2 Nov).
- Sound-alikes are original voices and licensed genre tracks. Never a real person's voice, and never a
  real film theme.
- Teasing only in the over-the-top tone, only about the person's habits with the dog (heartside
  DOG_VOICE, 2 Nov).

## Round 2 sound votes (2 Nov), what they add

- **Designed voices beat stock presets every time.** Krish rejected all 18 Higgsfield presets. He picked an
  ElevenLabs-designed voice for the narrator, golden, shepherd, doodle and chihuahua.
- **A comic character must sound like a cartoon, never a grown-up.** On the dachshund: "More personality,
  more pissed off, and a younger sounding voice", "Needs to be more like a cartoon character", and on the
  rejects "Sounds like a businessman", "Sounds lame and boring".
- **Music has to leave room for the voice and match the script.** On Catch Me: "Some of the layers in the
  track would be a bit too much for the voice." On Fool Me Twice: "Doesn't match the script." On The
  Ingenious, approved: "If there's no actual crescendo to this song, then maybe add in the crescendo from
  'Fool Me Twice.'"
- **The laughs live in the escalating middle.** On F02: "We need more humor and hilarity in some of the
  Google searches because that will be what makes people laugh. More time spent here". On F10: "More
  comedy and exaggerated hilarity". On F11: "More exaggerated hilarity".
- **A script must show what is heard and what is read.** On F05: "I don't see any script. I can't figure
  out if there's a voiceover or words on the screen". Every script now has separate columns for picture,
  on-screen text, and voiceover and sound.
- **The button can be a look.** On F01: "Have the dog on the mug wink at the end." That is done with the
  real dog, then a hard cut to the untouched product (his choice, 2 Nov).

## Round 3 (2 Nov)

- **Funny, never angry.** "It should be the kind of voice that's a bit miffed at the start, but then
  hilariously and positively takes control with hilarity, as opposed to this voice feeling like it has
  such an angry undertone." The comic arc is miffed, then scheming, then gleeful.
- **Motivate the turn.** On F02: "we probably need a connecting sentence for the dog to realize they need
  to get something." Every hero film needs the line where the dog decides to act.
- **Texting is its own register.** In an on-screen chat, the dogs text like people: LOL, emojis, bad
  spelling, WTF (his ruling, chat mocks only). Captions and voiceover stay clean.

## Round 4 (2 Nov)

- **The voice always sits on top.** The music swells only after the line lands ("drowns out the voice a
  little bit"). Sound effects fade in, never slam ("the sprinkle sound comes in really abruptly").
- **A flat read kills the joke.** On the judge: "Needs way more intonation and personality. It's so boring."
  Direct every character big, with a turn in the middle.
- **Cut a line that has lost its setup.** "The 'Nothing is free' line is now completely in the wrong place,
  just remove it entirely."

## Round 5 (2 Nov)

- **End on a landing, not a cliff.** "It just ends abruptly. Surely, we would just lower the music and have
  the sprinkle over it or something." Music dips under the final chime and fades out; it only cuts dead
  mid-film, on a punchline.

## Round 7 (2 Nov): the animatics were not funny enough

- **Gentle is not funny.** The wave-1 films were warm observational humour with sweet pictures. Krish: "its
  just not funny", "a lot more humour". The bar is now a laugh every 3 to 4 seconds, an escalation, a
  picture that is funny with the sound off, and the product as the last joke.
- **Four modes in rotation, each tuned separately:** absurd escalation, meme-text and TikTok voice,
  slapstick and visual gags, deadpan and dry wit. All are "Cheeky, still giftable". The rotation is how we
  flood the feeds with varied assets from one concept.

## 3 November (Krish on the roasts and the chat)

- **The dachshund's humour is the bar for roasts.** "These are not funny compared to the dash. Rewrite these in that kind of humour style." That means blunt, cheeky and a little gross, confessed with no shame: "I use them to fart on your pillow. My bad."
- **Troll-meme dogs, not plain cards.** "I want images that look like the dog is trolling them, like that classic troll meme image - but a dog. That vibe". Each slide is a real-looking dog with a wide, smug, squinting grin, in the scene of the line, with the line in classic outlined meme type. It is never a copy of the actual trollface artwork.
- **Let the dogs call each other out.** In the chat the joke lands when another dog corrects the deluded one: "Bro, that's a bone 😂", "They threw it AWAY mate 😂😵". Save the reveal for the punchline.
