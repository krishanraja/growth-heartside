# The funny bar: what Krish approved, and what it teaches

Read before drafting any funny-tone Heartside post. Source: his votes and comments on the idea board,
2 Nov 2026 (`docs/briefs/2026-11-01-ideas.json`), and his answers the same day (`CHANGES.md`).

He approved 10 of the 16 funny ideas and voted on none of the warm or pure-heart ones yet. Every comment
was about sound or comic execution: "the audio treatment and the specifics of the humor execution are
quite important to me".

## What a funny Heartside idea has

1. **A genre parody with a real sound.** Spy heist (F01, F03), wildlife documentary (F04), courtroom (F05),
   gallery critic (F07), action thriller (F12). The parody lives in the score and the voice, and the dog
   plays it completely straight.
2. **The dog's inner voice.** "What the dog's thinking in his head ... inner monologue" (F02). One voice
   per breed group, recurring, so each breed becomes a character (ruling, 2 Nov).
3. **Setup, escalation, button.** F01's setup is "a frame of the human ignoring the dog first". The
   escalation is the dog's wounded logic, and the button is the leap. The music swells into the button
   and cuts dead on it.
4. **Exaggeration you can see.** "Tiptoeing and comedy exaggeration" (F03), "obviously smug" (F07),
   "comedy caricature" (F10). A joke carried only by a caption fails.
5. **Music doing the acting.** "The music has to make this one" (F12), "comedy music" (F11).
6. **The product is the punchline.** Each one ends on the real design, labelled Example.

## Limits that stay

- The swear is bleeped, never spoken (ruling, 2 Nov).
- Sound-alikes are original voices and licensed genre tracks. Never a real person's voice, and never a
  real film theme.
- Teasing only in the over-the-top tone, only about the person's habits with the dog (heartside
  DOG_VOICE, 2 Nov).
