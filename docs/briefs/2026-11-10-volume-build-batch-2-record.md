## Lessons for batch 3 (observations, not Krish's rulings)

1. **The franchise that passed: the denial and the evidence.** The line is the dog's (or owner's) innocent claim; the
   picture is the evidence, absurd and on the dog's face, with a deadpan stare. Pie passed 9 and 9; the Samoyed reached
   9 once on the same structure. "Watch the turkey" sets up a task instead of a denial and stayed at 8.
2. **Welfare is now a tier 1 question.** Chocolate, cooked bones, toxic plants, live electrical cords and dogs in
   appliances are out, however funny.
3. **Fixing a familiar premise rarely lifts it.** The turkey went through three pictures and stayed at 8. Retire after
   one fix round unless the judges name a specific missing element.
4. **Motion from a start and end frame blurs the transition** (leaf pile, 2.0 to 3.0 s). Cut on either side of it.
5. **A judge read costs about 165k tokens.** Judge only what a producer's own look says could be a 9.
