# Voiceover scripts: the influencer-parody films (18 Nov 2026)

Krish, 18 Nov: "I want to generate a really heavy californian influencer accent voicing a full cringey script along to
these." The voice is the dog, played completely straight as a Californian lifestyle influencer: uptalk, vocal fry,
"like" and "literally" everywhere, breathy sincerity. The pictures stay real dogs doing real dog things; the gap
between the voice and the dog is the joke. House rules: no exclamation marks, no swearing, none of the banned worn
lines. Times are seconds into each cut film (`local/media/studio/<film>/<film>.mp4`); each line starts on its shot.

## As voiced (18 Nov, second read)

The first full reads ran 3 to 10 s longer than the films, so lines drifted up to 3.5 s off their shots. Each line was cut
to its shot length and read again in the same voice (HeyGen voice `bf05a110d2994891b32b873683a51283`, "valley girl").
"Starts" is where the line lands in `<film>_vo.mp4`; it never starts before its shot. Mixed at about -16 LUFS, peak -1.5 dBFS.
