## What to do for each post

Do this the same way as the F04, F05 and F12 motion reviews.

1. Start from the post's locked cut. Swap each still above for its clip, cut to the "Use" window and to the length the still held. Keep every on-screen line, the captions, the sound and the timing exactly as locked.
2. Review the whole cut at full frame rate, not only sampled frames. The checks were run on 6 frames per clip, so a flicker between them could have been missed. Check that:
   - the dog stays the same: breed, coat, markings and eyes;
   - the wardrobe stays the same: sweater, glasses, collar, bandana, bow tie and beanie;
   - the room stays the same;
   - no text, product, hand or extra limb is added;
   - no text that was in the frame changes;
   - no hard jump happens inside the window.
3. If a clip fails at full rate, keep that post's still for that shot and write down the second it fails at and why. Never stretch a window, slow a clip down or patch a clip to save it.
4. Export each motion cut beside its still version and put both on the owner board under **Motion candidates**, the same way as F04, F05 and F12. Each entry needs:
   - the post;
   - which shots moved;
   - the Runway task id and window of each moving shot;
   - your full-rate verdict;
   - the checksum of each file.
5. Stop there. Krish chooses motion or still and locks the post. Nothing goes to the Schedule or to Drive until he has locked it.
