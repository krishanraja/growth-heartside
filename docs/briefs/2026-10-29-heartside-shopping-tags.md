## What is wanted

Product tags on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and Pinterest posts for the mug, the blanket and the
portrait, landing on the right product page.

## Steps for the heartside session (Krish approves each account or permission change)

1. **Meta (Instagram and Facebook Shopping).** Install Shopify's Facebook & Instagram sales channel,
   connect the Heartside Facebook page and the `yourdoghasnotes` Instagram business account, sync the
   catalog, and submit the shop for Commerce review. Exclude the Artist's touch add-on from the
   catalog, since it cannot be bought alone.
2. **TikTok Shop (US).** Install Shopify's TikTok sales channel and connect the `yourdoghasnotes`
   account. Check first whether the account's London registration blocks US TikTok Shop; if it does,
   report that rather than registering a new account.
3. **Pinterest.** Install the Pinterest sales channel, claim heartside.io and turn on the catalog so
   product Pins are created from the products.
4. **Tracking.** Keep the store's own UTM handling as it is: the growth engine tags every link
   (`docs/ENGINE.md`, "Organic attribution"). Check that product-tag clicks arrive with the platform
   as referrer, which the engine counts as organic social.

## Done when

page with its price. Report what each channel needed, and anything that waits on Meta's or TikTok's
