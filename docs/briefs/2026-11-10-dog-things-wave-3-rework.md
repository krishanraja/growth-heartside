# Dog does dog things, wave 3 rework: every beat moves, nothing below 9

Krish, 10 Nov, on the board. On F48 and F49 (both No): "Should be a video, right?" On the three held reels (Yes):
"Rework these to be at least an 8, and then show me." He chose "Every beat moves" (about 475 Runway credits), then
added: "if you spend credits, nothing must be less than a 9".

So:
- every beat is a Runway clip (Gen-4 Turbo, 5 s, about 25 credits), made from the beat's checked still;
- each script is judged at tier 2 before any credit is spent, and only a 9 on paper is made;
- each finished reel is judged again, and only 9s go to Krish.

The humour core (docs/HUMOUR.md) is an intelligent observation, an utterly stupid premise and a slightly cruel payoff.
The three judges' notes on wave 3 are the brief:
- end on the cruel payoff, with the dog visibly in on it;
- a product ending must not steal the last laugh;
- the product's dog must match the reel's dog;
- every beat earns a laugh.
