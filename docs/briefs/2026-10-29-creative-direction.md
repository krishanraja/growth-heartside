# Creative direction: hero films and the daily shoppable layer

29 Oct 2026. Krish: "brainstorm art direction and creative concepts so that you can use Runway and
Higgsfield to build some incredible content to use as hero creatives, and then combine that with
absolutely hammering out easier-to-produce, viral, shareable content multiple times a day, all
shoppable". His answers the same day are recorded in `CHANGES.md`.

## What holds every piece

- AI makes the world: dogs, homes, light, motion. The product is always the real design: a live store
  render, or a heartside breed example with its painting composited unchanged (`AGENTS.md`, rule 4).
- No Buddy on camera until heartside approves an image of him.
- Every product image carries an Example label. The voice is heartside's: the dog talking to its
  person, warm first and funny second, no exclamation marks (`growth.py check`).
- Every piece declares its test (question, mechanism, control, one variable, signal) and its creative
  review (doctrine, alternatives, why it wins) before it is proposed to Krish.

## Art direction: real life, then the painting

- **The world.** Photographic, like a quiet film: warm late light, lived-in US homes, a low
  dog's-eye camera, shallow focus. Never cartoon, so AI footage is never mistaken for the gift.
- **The turn.** From that real moment to the painted gift. The default is a hard cut to the real
  render. One hero concept may instead morph a realistic AI dog into the real artwork, ending on the
  real render (Krish, 29 Oct: "could be cool, but that's just one creative idea").
- **Type.** Fraunces for the one big line; Courier Prime for the dog's own words; cream and ink, with
  stamp red once.
- **Sound.** The room first (keys, a tail on the floor), music second, licensed through Epidemic.

## The daily layer: 5 posts a day on each platform (Krish, 29 Oct)

The platforms are Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and Pinterest ("yes add pinterest daily", Krish, 29 Oct).
Pinterest is the only one with working product tags today, so every Pin tags the gift it shows and
links to that gift's page, tracked.

| Format | Made from | Shoppable through |
|---|---|---|
| "Translate your dog" carousel: one breed, four lines in its voice, the real gift last | Breed examples, cutouts | Product tag, Story sticker, Pin |
| Note still: a real gift and one line from the dog | Renders, breed mockups | Product tag, Pin |
| Story with a link sticker | Any of the above | Tracked link per creative |
| Cut-down of a hero (6 s, 15 s, still) | Hero films | Product tag |
| "Comment your dog's breed" with a reply in that breed's voice | Radar | The next carousel |

Twenty posts a day across four platforms is more than Krish can approve one by one, so they go to
him as one go-live batch a day on his Control Center list, and the daily pack trims to what fits his
time.

## Shoppable

Product tags need the Shopify catalog connected to Meta Commerce, TikTok Shop and Pinterest. Krish
handed that to a heartside build session (`docs/briefs/2026-10-29-heartside-shopping-tags.md`). Until
then, Facebook links, Story stickers and Pins carry the per-creative tags that `growth.py
attribution` reads.
