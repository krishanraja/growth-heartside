**Lessons:** the denial franchise lands at 8 by default and at 9 when the evidence is one clean, specific detail on
the face (cream on a nose, icing on a nose) beside an object whose damage is unmistakable (a crater in a pie, a missing
roof). Busy scenes and whole-body evidence (mud, flour) score lower. Judges ask the dog to be visibly in on it every
time.
