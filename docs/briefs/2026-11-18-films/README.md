## What to make next

1. Stage the joke in the picture, not only in the labels. The film judge wants an action whose consequence is visible: the dog that waited gets nothing, shown; a reaction shot from the dog the joke lands on.
2. Shoot dog behavior in its own setting. No prop that names a human activity: a yoga mat, a desk or a podium turns a real dog into a staged pose.
3. Give the end card a reason to be there, or put the product in the dog's world. A blanket on a cream card reads as a catalogue cut.
4. Judge the film, not the words: a 9 on words is a shortlist. The judge must always get the full live list (`jokelab.live_posts`).
5. Avoid object-on-object physics (a stick against rails): Kling fakes the collision.
