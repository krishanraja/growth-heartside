# Ten picture-first scripts. Judge each as the finished film: the picture as described, plus its few words.

The cast in every one: a gray and white scruffy terrier mix and a chocolate shaggy poodle and spaniel mix, both in orange collars, photoreal phone footage in their own home.

## c07 "Best mates"
Shots: Shot 1: both dogs sit side by side in a mess of torn stuffing on the living room floor, facing the lens. Shot 2: the terrier's head turns toward the poodle, then away, and he rises slowly and walks toward the edge of the frame without a sound. Shot 3 (payoff): the poodle alone on the floor in the mess, his head turning from the empty space where the terrier was to the lens.
Words: "Best mates."

## c04 "Leave it"
Shots: Shot 1: a single treat on the floor, terrier walking up, looking at it, then at the lens. Shot 2: terrier circles once and drops onto the exact spot, belly over the treat, eyes closed, chin raised. Shot 3 (payoff): poodle arrives, sniffs along the terrier's chest, sits and stares at the terrier's closed eyes while one of the terrier's eyes opens a crack.
Words: "Leave it." / "Left it."

## c01 "One toe on the floor"
Shots: Shot 1: wide living room, poodle sprawled on the couch facing the lens, terrier sitting on the rug below looking up at him. Shot 2: close on the poodle's back leg dangling over the edge, one toe resting on the floorboard, slow blink from the poodle. Shot 3 (payoff): wide side view, poodle full length on the couch, one toe on the floor, terrier's head turns from the toe up to the poodle's face and stays there.
Words: "No dogs on the couch." / "Technically on the floor."

## c03 "Do not wake him"
Shots: Shot 1: poodle asleep in the middle of the bed, terrier lying beside him, eyes open, watching the poodle's face. Shot 2: close, terrier's back legs press against the poodle's side, poodle slides an inch toward the edge, one ear flops over the edge, still asleep. Shot 3 (payoff): cut to the floor, poodle still asleep on the rug, terrier alone and spread out on the bed above, looking into the lens.
Words: "Do not wake him." / "Not once."

## c19 "Bed, couch, brother"
Shots: Shot 1: terrier lies on the dog bed, labeled first. Shot 2: terrier lies on the couch cushion. Shot 3 (payoff): the poodle lies on the couch, the terrier lies on top of him, and the poodle's flat eyes slide to the lens.
Words: "Bed." / "Couch." / "Brother."

## c25 "One eye open"
Shots: Shot 1: poodle asleep on the couch. Shot 2: close, one eye open, tracking. Shot 3 (payoff): the terrier walks toward the kitchen doorway, freezes under the eye, and backs away.
Words: "Sleeping with one eye open."

## c28 "The cold shoulder"
Shots: Shot 1: terrier facing away, poodle walks round the front. Shot 2: terrier turns away again. Shot 3 (payoff): both lying back to back, looking at the lens.
Words: "The cold shoulder."

## c30 "The naughty step"
Shots: Shot 1: the terrier walks to the stair. Shot 2: he sits on the second step facing the wall. Shot 3 (payoff): the poodle lies at the bottom looking at the lens.
Words: "The naughty step."

## c20 "Lap 40"
Shots: Shot 1: terrier runs a fast lap of the living room, poodle sitting in the middle. Shot 2: a blur of terrier circling, poodle lying down in the middle. Shot 3 (payoff): the poodle asleep in the middle with the terrier still circling in the background.
Words: "Lap 1" / "Lap 12" / "Lap 40"

## c26 "In the doghouse"
Shots: Shot 1: shredded cushion on the patio. Shot 2: the terrier walks into the doghouse. Shot 3 (payoff): the poodle sits beside the doghouse, the terrier's nose out of the doorway.
Words: "In the doghouse."
