# Ten gift-led scripts, round 2. Judge each as the finished film: the pictures as described, the labels, the caption.

The cast in every one: a gray and white scruffy terrier mix and a chocolate shaggy poodle and spaniel mix, both in orange collars, photoreal phone footage in their own home. The gift in the last shot is the real Heartside product, painted by the store from the dog's photo (a sherpa blanket printed with the dog's painted portrait, a painted portrait print, or an illustrated mug), shown as the store's own render; it is not drawn by AI.

## c09 "Photo for the portrait"
Shots: 1. First frame: a huge wet nose fills the lens. 2. Dog backs off, sniffs the lens again. 3. Turns his rear to the camera. 4. Walks out of frame. 5. Asleep, finally, head on paws. 6. Store render of the portrait print.
Labels: "Attempt 1: nose" / "Attempt 2: also nose" / "Attempt 3: back"
Caption: Portraits are painted from one photo. This is the one.

## c07 "Fastest dog on the street"
Shots: 1. Terrier mix lying on hardwood, paws twitch. 2. Legs paddle, a muffled huff. 3. Slides slightly, rotating on the floor. 4. Stops, snoring. 5. Poodle mix looks at him, sighs. 6. Store render of the blanket for grip. The slide on bare boards is why the blanket exists.
Labels: "Fastest dog on the street" / "He caught it"
Caption: He did not move. We checked.

## c13 "On guard"
Shots: 1. First frame: terrier mix sitting at forty five degrees, eyes shut. 2. Tilts further, jerks, recovers. 3. Tilts, slowly topples onto the rug. 4. Opens one eye, stares at lens. 5. Store render of the blanket for the landing.
Labels: "Still on duty" / "Not asleep"
Caption: Soft landing arranged.

## c05 "Buried on tile"
Shots: 1. Terrier mix noses at the floor beside a treat, pushing invisible dirt. 2. Again, harder, snout swiping. 3. Again on the rug, scraping. 4. He lies on the spot, proud. 5. Store render of the blanket: a surface that finally takes a burial.
Labels: "Burial attempt: tile" / "Burial attempt: rug" / "Burial attempt: still tile"
Caption: Found him a surface that takes it.

## c12 "Sprawl creep"
Shots: 1. Terrier mix in a tight loaf on a small plain bed. 2. Stretches, two paws off. 3. Sprawled, head off. 4. Starfish, bed reduced to a pillow for one foot. 5. Store render of the larger blanket.
Labels: "Loaf" / "Puddle" / "Pancake"
Caption: Needed a bigger one.

## c15 "Furniture"
Shots: 1. Poodle mix lays chin on the terrier mix. 2. Head on the flank. 3. Whole body slides on. 4. Terrier stares at the lens, immobile. 5. Store render of the blanket for the poodle's own pillow.
Labels: "I am not furniture"
Caption: He needed his own.

## c16 "You made your bed"
Shots: 1. Terrier mix digs at a plain bed. 2. Rotates, digs again. 3. Mound formed. 4. Lies in it. 5. Store render of the blanket.
Labels: "You made your bed" / "Now lie in it"
Caption: Made him a better one.

## c14 "Under it"
Shots: 1. Terrier mix shoves head under a plain throw on the sofa. 2. Back half under. 3. Only a lump moving across the cushions. 4. Poodle mix sniffs the lump, lump grumbles. 5. Store render of the blanket.
Labels: "He is under the blanket" / "He has been under the blanket" / "He is the blanket"
Caption: Lump approved.

## c06 "The eyes follow you"
Shots: 1. Terrier mix curled, apparently asleep, eyes shut. 2. Camera moves left; one eye opens a slit and tracks it. 3. Camera moves right; both eyes slit, follow. 4. Poodle mix does the same from the next room's doorway. 5. Store render of the portrait print.
Labels: "Something is watching" / "It is not asleep"
Caption: The portrait takes the next shift.

## c10 "Monkey see"
Shots: 1. Terrier mix sniffs a spot, poodle mix sniffs the same spot. 2. Terrier turns once, lies; poodle turns once, lies. 3. Terrier stands, walks into the doorframe edge; poodle follows and does the same. 4. Terrier looks back and sees it copying. 5. Store render of the blanket, one for each.
Labels: "He has to have everything I have" / "Even this"
Caption: So we needed two.
