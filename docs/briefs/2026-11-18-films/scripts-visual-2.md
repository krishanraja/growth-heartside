# Ten picture-first scripts, round 2. Judge each as the finished film: the picture as described, plus its few words.

The cast in every one: a gray and white scruffy terrier mix and a chocolate shaggy poodle and spaniel mix, both in orange collars, photoreal phone footage in their own home.

## c07 "Customs"
Shots: Shot 1: the dogs meet in the kitchen, the terrier begins the greeting sniff at the poodle's rear. Shot 2: the poodle steps one pace sideways, still standing, so its head is over the terrier's bowl. Payoff: wide shot, a straight line: terrier nose on poodle's rear, poodle's head in the terrier's bowl, both busy; the poodle's eyes cut to the lens.
Words: "Customs."

## c25 "Nothing gets past him"
Shots: Shot 1: the terrier stands at the window, chest out. Shot 2: wide, the poodle is behind him at the bowl. Payoff: the poodle licks the bowl, walks away; the terrier turns and sees the empty bowl.
Words: "Nothing gets past him."

## c11 "Due diligence"
Shots: Shot 1: the terrier sniffs crumb one, moves on. Shot 2: he sniffs crumb two at length. Shot 3: he lines up on crumb three, nose inches away. Payoff: the poodle walks in from the side of frame and eats it; the terrier lifts his head slowly and looks at the poodle, then the lens.
Words: "Due diligence completed."

## c16 "Equal split"
Shots: Shot 1: two bowls, each dog at its own. Shot 2: halfway, the terrier steps to the poodle's bowl while the poodle steps to his, crossing. Shot 3: they swap again. Payoff: both bowls empty, both lying flat with round bellies, looking at each other.
Words: "Everything is split evenly."

## c20 "Waiting for the vote"
Shots: Shot 1: the poodle sits at his full bowl, tail wagging, not eating, looking at the terrier. Shot 2: the terrier looks at the lens, uninterested. Shot 3: the terrier walks over. Payoff: the terrier eats the poodle's food while the poodle sits and looks at him.
Words: "He is waiting for the vote."

## c02 "Adjacent, not near"
Shots: Shot 1: the poodle mix eating from its bowl. Shot 2: the terrier lies flat on the floor, chin down, six inches off, eyes locked on the bowl. Shot 3: the terrier inches forward on his belly, still lying. Payoff shot: close on the terrier's nose resting on the rim of the bowl, eyes up at the lens; the poodle glances down at him and keeps eating.
Words: "He is not near the bowl. He is adjacent."

## c18 "Not looking"
Shots: Shot 1: the poodle eats. Shot 2: the terrier sits head turned hard away. Shot 3: close on his eyes cutting sideways to the bowl. Payoff: the poodle stops eating and stares at the terrier; the terrier looks harder at the wall.
Words: "Not looking."

## c23 "Undefeated"
Shots: Shot 1: the two dogs stare at each other, still. Shot 2: the terrier looks away and walks out. Shot 3: the poodle stays locked on the empty spot, eyes watering. Payoff: he finally blinks hard and looks at the lens.
Words: "Undefeated."

## c21 "Senior dog, by four months"
Shots: Shot 1: the poodle sits tall, chest out, chin up. Shot 2: the terrier walks in and sniffs his rear. Payoff: close on the poodle's face, composure folding, eyes sliding to the lens, still sitting.
Words: "Senior dog."

## c01 "Arrived second, on purpose"
Shots: Shot 1 (0-2s): the poodle mix already has its head in the bowl, eating. Shot 2: the terrier trots in and stops two feet away, sits very upright, looks at the lens. Shot 3: the poodle lifts its head, licks its lips, walks off. Payoff shot: the terrier still sitting upright at the bowl, looks down into it, looks up at the lens.
Words: "He chose to arrive second."
