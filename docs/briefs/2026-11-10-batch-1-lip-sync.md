# Batch 1: the human-arms lip-syncs (S12, HAL-02 to HAL-07)

Sprint 120, posting 12 and 13 November (`docs/plans/2026-11-10-sprint-120.md`). The format is Krish's pick of
10 Nov ("This is Good"): YouTube Short `u7s47cIeh6M`, "My dog's 9 to 5", 18.8M views when read. A dog in a hoodie,
with a hidden person's arms as its hands, acts out each line of a song everyone knows, one beat every 1 to 2
seconds, with the line on screen.

**What makes the reference work** (read frame by frame, 10 Nov): the premise card and the gag are both in frame 0;
the song is already in the viewer's head; every beat is literal, so the joke is the dog doing exactly what the line
says; the arms make people look twice; it ends on the biggest visual gag, held.

**How we make it.**
- **Character.** One dog per post, made once as a reference still and reused as the reference for every shot,
  so the coat, collar and hoodie never change (floor `generation-continuity`).
- **Shots.** One still per beat, made on Higgsfield. Motion comes from Runway image-to-video at 2 to 4 seconds a
  shot: the hands do the action and the dog's head reacts, with every movement given a cause (floor
  `real-behavior-in-motion`).
- **Cut.** Cut to the track's beat. Each sung line goes on screen in the house caption style, clear of the dog's
  face (`text-clear-of-faces`).
- **Song.** Added by the publishing desk in the app at posting, from the start point named below, so the platform
  links it. The picture is cut to a guide copy of the track that never leaves the machine.
- **Signature ending (S12 only).** A hard cut to the store's own mug render held 3 seconds or more. The link and the
  Christmas date (4 December) go in the caption.

Lyrics are staged beat by beat as the song is sung. Each beat below is described in our words; the on-screen line is
the sung line at that moment.

## S12: My dog's Christmas shopping (signature, ends on the real mug)

- **Song:** "All I Want for Christmas Is You", Mariah Carey. Start at the slow opening; cut to the beat from the
  first chorus.
- **Dog:** a black lab in a red hoodie. No chestnut or red dachshund (it reads as Buddy).
- **Premise card, frame 0:** "My dog's Christmas shopping".
- **Beats:**
  1. Slow intro: the lab on the sofa by the tree, the human hands writing a very long wish list.
  2. The list unrolls off the sofa and across the floor.
  3. In the car, the hands on the wheel, the lab staring straight ahead, serious.
  4. In a store aisle, the hands pushing a cart while the lab inspects one item and rejects it.
  5. In the toy aisle, the hands put back a squeaky toy; the lab watches it go.
  6. At home, the hands wrap a box badly; tape ends up on the lab's ear.
  7. The lab writes the card, the hands holding the pen; a paw print is pressed beside the signature.
  8. On the payoff line, the lab turns to camera and the hands hold out the wrapped gift.
  9. Hard cut to the real mug, held 3 seconds or more.
- **Comment prompt:** "What's on your dog's list? Wrong answers only."
