## Route

1. Profile conversion. Prepare a concrete bio and pinned-content brief explaining why dog owners should follow for recurring humour, while keeping the gift proposition and Heartside.io destination. Creative prep owns execution; changes await exact approval. Capture before/after follower count, profile visits and follows where exposed.
2. Timely comments. Scout 12 fresh eligible US dog-owner/community channels. Shortlist posts by age, recent activity and a natural dog-owner opening; collect two timestamped activity readings where possible. Prefer under two hours old as a test hypothesis; do not claim an algorithmic guarantee. Do not select by account size or lifetime likes alone.
3. Bounded discovery pilot. Propose 8 exact comments across at least four fresh channels over 48 hours. Max three per channel across the pilot, replies included; record counts and previous use. No sales posts, fundraising pitches or product-led ambush. Approve exact wording and destination before sending. Read at 2h and 24h for comment likes/replies, profile visits/follows where available. Follower changes with overlapping owned/paid activity are directional, not attributed.
4. Shared distribution. Prepare three named partnership packets for US dog communities, events or dog creators whose audience fits and whose terms can be no-fee. Offer a permission-based humorous feature using their dog/clip, with credit and proposed coauthorship. No purchased follower guarantee, gift spending or unsolicited publication. Creative prep makes the chosen asset, Scheduling releases it after approval. Official Collabs mechanism can distribute accepted coauthored content to both audiences: https://about.fb.com/news/2023/08/music-and-collabs-on-instagram/
5. Non-follower content distribution. Propose to creative prep/Scheduling one approved humour asset as a Trial Reel only if the actual account exposes eligibility. Meta documents non-follower-first distribution, not a guaranteed result: https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfoms-best/ . Feature availability not checked for Heartside. No new publishing authority.
6. Selection. Judge by evidence of new relevant followers per hour of acquisition work, plus profile visits and follows per 100 visits where fields and matching windows exist. Comment popularity is diagnostic. Review after 48h and after first collaboration's 72h report. If comments get attention but no profile visits, revise venue/opening; if visits arrive without follows, revise profile promise/content fit. A small or unexposed denominator remains inconclusive. Reallocate effort toward the route showing actual follower conversion, not approved jokes. US audience claims need audience evidence.
